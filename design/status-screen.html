MD3 Status Screen

The one screen the open firmware shows. It's dark until you press the menu button, then the active preset's curve sits in the middle, the stream on top, and volume around the edge. Drawn at the panel's real 240 × 240.

Try it

The first Menu press wakes the screen, and each one after that moves to the next preset. With no press for 8 s, the screen goes dark.

What's on it

CentreThe preset's curve, 168 px wide. The 8 built-in filters show their impulse response: their frequency responses are nearly identical below 20 kHz, and what actually tells them apart is pre-ringing, post-ringing and the timing of the peak. Custom presets (Warm, Bright) show their frequency response from 1 kHz to 20 kHz on a log axis, since their whole point is a tilt.
Under the curveThe preset's name and a short note: "Minimum phase · fast", "Linear · apodizing", "Warm · −2 dB at 10 kHz".
TopThe stream: rate and format ("96 kHz PCM", "DSD128"), from the Savitech link. Bit depth isn't shown, because the link doesn't carry it.
EdgeVolume as an arc from 7 to 5 o'clock, with the dB value at the bottom. While it's changing, the arc brightens and the number shows big for 1.5 s.
Corner tagGain (Low/High), at 2 o'clock, in a small size.
Wake and sleepThe screen is dark until you press Menu. Each press after that moves to the next preset, and the curve morphs over 250 ms. The volume keys don't wake it. After 8 s with no press: backlight off, GC9A01 sleep-in, and LVGL stopped.
Drawing costOne full redraw per press, plus 250 ms of morph frames. Then nothing until the next event, so nothing animates while you just listen.

The curves here are illustrative shapes, not ESS's coefficients. The firmware will draw the real ones: impulse responses traced from the datasheet's plots (ES9039Q2M v0.2.3, Digital Filters section) for F1–F8, and the computed response of our own coefficients for the custom presets. Preset order and names follow FILTER_SHAPE 0–7.
