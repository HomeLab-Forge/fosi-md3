MD3 Status Screen

Draft for firmware 0.3.1. The screen is dark until you press Menu. Then the active EQ preset's curve sits in the middle, the stream and gain are at the top, and the volume runs around the edge. Drawn at the panel's real 240 × 240, with the firmware's own positions and curves.

What's on it

TopThe stream ("44.1 kHz PCM", "DSD128", "no stream"), and under it, centred and smaller, "High Gain" or "Low Gain".
CentreThe preset's frequency response, 200 Hz to 20 kHz on a log axis, ±7 dB at 6 px per dB. These are the firmware's own curves: each preset's smooth target, which the real filter matches within 0.24 dB at 44.1/48 kHz.
Under the curveThe preset's name and a short note ("+6 dB below 1 kHz").
EdgeVolume as an amber arc from 7 to 5 o'clock, with the dB value at the very bottom.
Above the dBEstimated draw, averaged since plug-in, in watts and in % of an iPhone 15 Pro battery per hour. The firmware counts exact time in each state (screen, amps, DAC muted, stream) and multiplies by datasheet figures plus a fixed part for the USB bridge and converters, calibrated to the iOS measurement. An estimate, roughly ±25%.
SleepMenu turns it off, or 8 s without a press does.

Gain is digital: Low is 6 dB of attenuation in the DAC, the same as the stock firmware.
